# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, deployed to Vercel. Chosen by the user in the 2026-10-09 interview. Contact form sends via Resend (key pending).

## Users

Two buyer groups, weighted equally:

1. **Enterprise ERP buyers.** CFOs, CIOs, finance-systems and IT heads at mid-to-large companies evaluating an Oracle ERP implementation, migration, extension or support partner. They are conservative, trust-first, and usually compare several vendors.
2. **Mid-market and startup AI buyers.** Founders, operations and CX leaders who want AI agents, automation, chatbots or copilots that hold up in production.

Secondary: prospective hires (careers page).

## Product Purpose

This is the company website of Nanhi AI Mindforge, a technology consultancy. It must make a first-time visitor understand what the firm does, believe it is a competent and legitimate partner with global reach, and start a conversation through the contact form.

## Positioning

AI engineering and Oracle ERP under one roof. The firm implements and runs the ERP and then layers agents and automation on top, so one partner covers both the system of record and the intelligence on top of it. The delivery stance is production-first: grounded outputs, runs in the client's stack, human-in-the-loop, and measured by outcomes.

## Operating Context

Buyers arrive from referrals, LinkedIn, search or outbound emails. They skim the services, look for evidence of competence and legitimacy (address, legal entity, method, security stance), and then either email or fill in the contact form. The engagement starts with a discovery call.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: AI agents and agentic workflows; intelligent automation/RPA; conversational AI; generative AI and copilots (RAG); predictive analytics/ML; computer vision and document AI; custom app development; AI strategy and consulting; Oracle ERP implementation, cloud migration, custom extensions, Finance/HCM/SCM integrations, AI on ERP data, managed support.
- Pages: Home, Services (AI, Oracle ERP, App Development, Consulting), Industries, Approach, About, Careers, Contact, Privacy, Terms.
- Not in v1: chatbot, blog/insights, team profiles, CMS.
- Undecided (owner to supply): legal entity name, registered street address, CIN/GST, contact inbox, domain, Resend key.

## Brand Commitments

- Name is written **Nanhi AI Mindforge**, with "AI" in capitals.
- The "forge" metaphor from the prototype may be kept in the copy, used sparingly.
- No logo exists; a typographic wordmark and monogram are to be designed.
- Visual direction pinned by the user: **refined enterprise**. Restrained and editorial, credible to a CFO and still sharp to a startup founder.
- No people on the site; everything is in the company's voice.
- HQ: Delhi NCR, India, serving clients globally.

## Evidence on Hand

None. The firm is new, with no public clients, testimonials, logos, certifications, partnerships or metrics. **Never fabricate these.** The prototype's testimonials and vendor-logo strip are invented and must not return. Technologies can be presented as "what we build with", never as partnerships. Illustrative use-cases must be framed as examples ("for instance…"), never as delivered results.

## Product Principles

1. Earn trust through clarity and specificity, not borrowed proof.
2. Each service line must stand on its own for its own buyer.
3. Every claim is one the firm can defend on a sales call.
4. Global posture, Indian roots: name the real HQ and describe the delivery model honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. The site must work fully at phone width and with reduced motion.
